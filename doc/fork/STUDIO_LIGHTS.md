# Studio lights

Eight more lights for Solid shading, in **Lighting ▸ Studio** of the Viewport Shading popover:

| Light | Look |
|---|---|
| soft | One large soft light from the upper left, a faint fill and a thin rim |
| clay | Even light from above that wraps around the form, for sculpting |
| warm_cool | Warm light from the right, cool blue fill from the left, a blue rim from behind |
| backlit | Bright edges on both sides, a dim front |
| neon | Magenta from the left, cyan from the right |
| product | A sharp light with strong highlights and a dark fill, to show dents and bumps |
| moonlight | Cool blue light from the upper right, a dark front and a faint warm bounce from below |
| top | Warm light from straight above, like the sun at noon |

They're plain `.sl` files in `release/datafiles/studiolights/studio`, made by
`icon_work/make_studio_lights.py`, where each one is listed as up to four lights.
